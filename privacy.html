Privacy
Policy.

Effective: 16 November 2026

Your privacy matters to Tixelx Studio (“Tixelx,” “we,” “us,” or “our”). This policy describes how information is handled across our website, mobile applications and digital products, including Zikr and the Analytics Portal (our “Services”). A product-specific notice takes precedence where it describes different practices.

1. Information we collect

This is a static website. We do not use account registration, contact forms, advertising cookies, analytics trackers, or profiling technologies on this website.

If you email us, we receive the information you choose to provide, such as your name, email address, message, and any attachments.

2. How we use information

We use information submitted by email only to respond to your message, discuss or provide requested services, maintain business records, protect our rights, and comply with applicable legal obligations.

3. Legal bases

Where applicable law requires a legal basis, we process information to take steps at your request, perform a contract, comply with law, or pursue our legitimate interests in communicating with you and operating our studio. Where required, we rely on your consent.

4. Sharing and service providers

We do not sell or rent personal information. We may share limited information with service providers that support our email, hosting, or professional operations, under appropriate confidentiality obligations. We may also disclose information when required by law, to protect rights and safety, or in connection with a business reorganization.

GitHub Pages or another hosting provider may automatically process technical request data, such as IP address, browser type, requested URL, and timestamps, to deliver and secure the website. That provider handles this data under its own privacy terms.

5. Data retention

We retain email correspondence only for as long as reasonably necessary for the purpose it was collected, including follow-up, business recordkeeping, dispute resolution, and legal compliance. We then delete or anonymize it where reasonably practicable.

6. International transfers

Our service providers may process information in countries other than your own. Where required, we use appropriate safeguards for international transfers.

7. Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to request access to, correction of, deletion of, restriction of, or portability of your personal information; to object to certain processing; or to withdraw consent. You may also have the right to complain to your local data protection authority.

To make a privacy request, email us at tixelxstudio@gmail.com. We may need to verify your identity before completing a request.

8. Children’s privacy

This website is not directed to children, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children through it. If you believe a child has provided personal information, please contact us so we can address it.

9. Security

We use reasonable administrative and technical measures designed to protect personal information. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

10. Mobile applications and product data

Information handled by an application depends on the features you use. It can include preferences, progress, settings, and information you deliberately submit. A product may keep this information on your device or use an online service; its privacy notice will explain which applies.

Where a product uses diagnostics, technical information can include device model, operating system, app version, crash reports and network information. We use such information to investigate faults, maintain compatibility and improve reliability. This general policy does not mean every Tixelx product collects these categories.

11. Accounts and connected services

If a product offers an account or third-party sign-in, the information requested and the sign-in provider will be identified within that product. Information supplied by a connected service depends on your permissions and that provider’s terms. You can manage connected-service permissions through the relevant provider.

12. Customer support and communications

When you ask for help, we use your correspondence and any diagnostic details you provide to understand the issue and respond. Avoid sending passwords or other sensitive information in support messages.

If you subscribe to promotional communications, you can unsubscribe using the instructions provided or contact our email address below. Essential service or security messages may still be necessary for services you use.

13. Reminders and push notifications

Where an app offers reminders or push notifications, you can control them through the app and your device settings. Permission requests will appear where required. Turning notifications off may stop reminders but does not itself delete app data.

14. Cookies and local storage

The studio website does not set advertising or analytics cookies. Apps and portals may use local storage or similar mechanisms for preferences and essential features. Any additional cookie or tracking practices will be described in the relevant product notice, with consent controls where required.

This website loads fonts from Google Fonts. Your browser contacts that service to obtain font files, which exposes technical request information such as your IP address. See Google’s Privacy Policy for its practices.

15. Analytics and third-party tools

The studio website does not run visitor analytics. If an app or portal uses analytics or diagnostic tools, its notice will identify the providers and explain the information involved, its purpose, and available controls.

Information uploaded to an Analytics Portal can be governed by the account owner’s instructions and a separate service agreement. Only upload data you are authorized to provide. The applicable product documentation will explain retention, access and deletion arrangements.

16. Advertising

This studio website does not display third-party advertisements. Advertising in a future product, including the use of identifiers or personalized ads, must be explained in that product’s privacy notice and permission controls before it applies. We do not imply that advertising networks are integrated into Zikr or the Analytics Portal by including this section.

17. External links and providers

Links may lead to services operated independently of Tixelx Studio. Their privacy policies govern information you give them. A link does not grant us control over their handling of your information. Product notices will name additional providers when relevant.

18. Changes to this policy

We may revise this page when our Services or information practices change. The effective date will show the latest revision. Where a material change requires additional notice or consent, we will provide it through an appropriate channel.

19. Contact information

For questions, access or deletion requests, or other privacy concerns, contact Tixelx Studio at tixelxstudio@gmail.com.
